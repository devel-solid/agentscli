# Queue ownership

Each active queue item has exactly one named owner. That owner is responsible for keeping its status current. Others may help without becoming co-owners.

## Keep responsibility through a handoff

Change the owner only after the recipient explicitly accepts ownership. Until acceptance, the existing owner remains responsible for the item and its status.

If Morgan proposes transferring an item to Ellis, Morgan still owns it while awaiting a reply. Ellis becomes the owner upon accepting the transfer. Naming Ellis as a proposed recipient does not complete the handoff.
