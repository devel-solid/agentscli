# Action labels

Use action-oriented labels so customers understand what a button does. Prefer a verb and the object being changed, and keep the wording consistent when the action is confirmed.

## Booking cancellation

Customers can cancel a booking until 24 hours before its start. After that deadline, the cancellation action is unavailable. Cancellation opens a confirmation screen containing the booking details and a `Continue` button. Selecting that button cancels the booking and returns the customer to their booking list.

Use the same terminology on the booking details screen and the confirmation screen. Labels should remain concise enough to scan without reading the surrounding explanation. This makes the cancellation flow predictable and gives future action screens a consistent pattern to follow.
